# Claim compilation rules

## A claim is not a topic

`Pricing` is a topic. `Azad is priced below incumbent Western suppliers for comparable compressor-airfoil work` is a claim

## Support, contradiction, qualification

- **Supporting** evidence asserts materially the same proposition under aligned scope
- **Contradicting** evidence asserts the opposite under materially aligned scope
- **Qualifying** evidence narrows, conditions, explains, or adds an exception without directly opposing the proposition

## Scope before contradiction

Before classifying a disagreement, compare:

- period and forecast horizon
- geography
- product, platform, part, or service
- direct customer and end customer
- new-build, aftermarket, upgrade, or repair
- transaction stage
- value versus volume
- current versus historical versus target state
- numerator, denominator, currency, and unit
- first-part qualification versus serial-production approval versus mature supplier status

Use `scoped-difference` when the apparent conflict is explained by one or more of these dimensions

## Numerical evidence

Do not average numbers unless scopes and definitions are aligned. Prefer a range with source explanation. Flag denominator or methodology conflicts explicitly

## Source independence

Several evidence records from one call count as one source. A follow-up call from the same expert is not fully independent corroboration. Duplicate documents and derivative summaries do not add weight

## Synthesis language

Use calibrated language:

- `The evidence consistently indicates...`
- `Most sources support..., while...`
- `Evidence is mixed because...`
- `The difference appears attributable to...`
- `The point remains unresolved because...`

Do not claim external truth. This system synthesizes expert evidence
